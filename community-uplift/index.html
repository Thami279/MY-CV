Community Uplift

Empowering rural classrooms with skilled volunteers, open educational resources, and sustainable technology.

Explore our programs

Why we exist

Many rural learners face limited access to qualified teachers, updated learning materials, and mentorship. Community Uplift bridges that gap by connecting professionals, students, and creators with schools that could use an extra hand.

Volunteer Network

Medical, engineering, and creative professionals offer weekly remote sessions covering STEM, health, and the arts.

Resource Library

We curate open-licensed lesson plans, coding challenges, and reading materials tailored to South African curricula.

Tech Bridges

Partner organisations help install low-power computer labs and introduce AI tools that run offline.

Flagship programs

Future Coders Club

Saturday workshops introducing Grade 8–11 learners to HTML, CSS, and Python using offline-first curricula.

  • 12 partner schools in KwaZulu-Natal
  • Mentors drawn from local universities
  • Project-based learning with community themes

STEM on Wheels

A mobile lab that brings science experiments, 3D printing demos, and robotics kits to remote classrooms every month.

  • 20 rural stops per quarter
  • Hands-on sessions for both learners and teachers
  • Offline video library available after each visit

Health Voices

Healthcare professionals host bilingual webinars on wellness, nutrition, and careers in health sciences.

  • Monthly Q&A panels
  • Community radio partnership for wider reach
  • Resource packs translated into isiZulu and isiXhosa
